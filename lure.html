Compressor New

LURE

Two kinds of compressor in one rack. A fast FET compressor catches the peaks, then a smooth optical leveler evens out the level. Use both, one alone, or swap their order.

  • FET compressor: attack from about 0.1 to 2 ms, release from 45 ms to 1.1 s, ratios 4, 8, 12 and 20, and all buttons in
  • Ratios tighten on held notes, and the release slows after long passages and keeps a little grip between phrases, as on classic units of this kind
  • Optical leveler: Compress or Limit; it holds a vocal or a mix firmly, lets a short hit go in milliseconds and a long passage in seconds
  • Emphasis, a sidechain high-pass and stereo link, so bass does not pump it
  • Mix for parallel compression; swap the order of the two units with one click
  • Two VU meters with real VU movement, showing gain reduction or output
  • 15 presets, oversampling up to 8x with its small latency reported to your DAW, resizable interface
