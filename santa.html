Stereo chorus pedal

SANTA

A lush stereo chorus in a pedal you can actually use: three knobs, a footswitch, and Santa's nose. Click it and it glows red and adds warm tube drive.

  • Three-voice stereo ensemble with a slow random drift: wide, smooth and never seasick
  • Lows stay dry and tight; the chorus is rounded off like a vintage analog chip
  • Rate, Depth and Mix, plus a footswitch with a status LED
  • Tube drive on the nose: soft warmth on quiet parts, real crunch when you hit it
  • Zero latency, resizable interface
