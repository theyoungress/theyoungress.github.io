MBAND

A multiband host: split the sound into up to five frequency bands and run your own VST3 effects on each. Saturate only the lows, put reverb only on the highs, compress only the mids.

  • Two to five bands, up to four of your own VST3 effects on each. MBAND has no effects of its own
  • Zero-latency crossovers, or linear phase with no phase change at all
  • The latency of every effect is compensated, so the bands stay in time
  • Mix, trim, solo and mute per band; 2x and 4x oversampling for the effects
  • 32 macros your DAW can automate: press Learn and move a control in any loaded effect
  • Split, drag and type crossovers on the spectrum; drag effects between bands
  • Scans your effects in a program of its own; one that crashes in its audio code is taken out and the rest plays on
  • Presets with the effects' own settings, a dark and a light look, resizable window
