De-esser New

SPLIT

A de-esser that listens for the balance between the S band and the rest of the voice, not for level. A whisper and a shout are treated the same, so there is no threshold to chase: set how much it may take and how hard it gets there, and every S comes down by the same measure.

  • Key filter, high pass or band pass, from 500 Hz to 15 kHz with FOCUS; KEY LISTEN to hear exactly what it hears
  • RANGE up to 36 dB (it never takes more), RATIO 2:1 to 20:1, RELEASE 1 to 100 ms
  • HF ONLY turns down only the highs, and SHIFT moves that band up to an octave either way; or let the whole voice dip
  • A 1.15 ms look-ahead catches the start of every S; steady hiss and room noise between words are left alone
  • A live display: the key filter, the reduction across the spectrum as it happens, stereo input and output meters and gain reduction
  • 16 presets for vocals, speech, instruments and the mix bus, A/B and undo
