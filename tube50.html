Valve saturator New

TUBE 50

Fifty valves in one plug-in. Each sits in a circuit of its own kind, simulated sample by sample, so every valve reacts to your signal in its own way. Pick one, turn DRIVE.

  • 24 small-signal valves (12AX7, 6SN7, 6922, EF86 and more) and 26 output valves (6L6, EL34, EL84, KT88, 300B, 845 and more)
  • Triode and pentode gain stages; single-ended and push-pull output stages with a transformer
  • Three knobs: DRIVE, AMOUNT from dry to valve, and TONE
  • Browser with PREAMP, DRIVER and POWER tabs, search and favourites; click-free valve changes
  • BIAS, SUPPLY SAG, AUTO GAIN, A and B, 15 presets, oversampling up to 8x; zero latency, resizable interface
