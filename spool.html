Tape keyboard New

SPOOL

A keyboard that plays tapes, built like the tape-replay keyboards of the 1960s and 70s. Every key has its own 8-second length of tape with three sounds side by side on it; press a key and the tape runs past the head, let go and it springs home.

  • Three tracks on every tape: the TRACK selector (or the mod wheel) moves the head from A to B to C, or plays all three
  • Notes run out after about 8 seconds, as on the old machines, or loop them; a key pressed before its tape is home starts part-way in
  • Wow, flutter, per-note tape saturation, hiss and hum, key and tape mechanics, adjustable tape return
  • 26 sounds made from scratch (flutes, strings, choirs, brass, organs, keys and more), plus your own WAV, AIFF or FLAC with loops, envelope and Stretch
  • Four pedal slots with twelve effects, a combo amp or rotary speaker, a room, an LFO and two modulation routes
  • 53 presets in 11 categories; open the lid for the engine
