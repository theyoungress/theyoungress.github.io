TRINITY

Pitch correction that goes from natural to hard-locked.

Classic follows the singer and leaves the expression in. Underground snaps every note to the key for the locked sound modern rap vocals are built on. Either way, formant-preserving correction keeps the voice sounding like the person who sang it.

Set the key and scale on the keyboard, pick a vocal range, and watch the pitch path show what came in, where it's headed and what came out.

  • Classic and Underground tuning styles
  • Retune speed, correction, humanize, transition, tracking and sustain
  • Pitch, formant and mix shaping
  • HQ (10 ms) or Low Latency (3 ms) for recording through it

IRON & GLASS

A British console preamp and EQ, into a tube optical compressor.

Iron

The console half. A Class-A preamp with transformer iron you can drive from clean to thick, then an EQ with curves fitted to the original circuit: air at 12 kHz, a sweepable mid from 360 Hz to 7.2 kHz, a low band from 35 to 220 Hz, and a high-pass filter.

Glass

A tube optical compressor that holds a vocal steady without squashing it. Pick Fixed, Manual or Fix/Man timing, blend it in with the mix knob, and keep the low end from pumping it with the sidechain high-pass. Put it before or after the EQ.

  • Mic and line input modes
  • VU meter for input, gain reduction and output
  • Up to 8x oversampling
  • A/B compare and presets

AIR

One knob of air that never turns harsh.

AIR lifts the top of a vocal, mix or instrument with a dynamic shelf set above the harsh zone, and adds an octave-up sheen to sources that came in dull. Built-in smoothing gives esses and spikes less lift than everything else, so pushing it harder only gets smoother.

  • 16 presets for vocals, mix bus, master and instruments
  • Click-free preset switching
  • Live AIR and SMOOTH meters

I paid. Where's my plug-in?

Install Young Ress Hub and sign in with the email you used at checkout, or create an account with it and enter the code we email you. The plug-in is waiting in your library: click Install. Your receipt comes from Lemon Squeezy.

I used a different email at checkout.

Email theyoungress@gmail.com with the order number from your receipt and the email of your Hub account, and we'll move the plug-in over.

How many computers can I use?

Each plug-in activates on up to 3 of your computers. To free a slot, deactivate a computer on the Hub's Account page.

Which DAWs and systems work?

Any DAW that loads VST3 plug-ins on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). TRINITY also runs on macOS as VST3 and AU, so it works in Logic Pro. Mac versions of IRON & GLASS and AIR are in progress.

My computer warns me about the installer.

The Hub isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway once.

Can I get a refund?

If a plug-in doesn't work on a computer that meets its requirements and we can't fix it, contact us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund you. The details are in the Terms of Service.

Who handles the payment?

Lemon Squeezy is the seller of record. It takes the payment, adds any sales tax or VAT for your country and sends your receipt. Young Ress never sees your card details.
