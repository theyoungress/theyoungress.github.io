Pitch correction

TRINITY

Pitch correction that goes from natural to hard-locked, with formant-preserving correction that keeps the voice sounding like the singer.

  • Classic and Underground tuning styles
  • Live pitch display, with waveform and spectrum views
  • MIDI target notes: play the notes to tune to
  • Retune speed, correction, humanize, transition, tracking and sustain
  • Pitch, formant and mix shaping
  • Key, scale, A4 reference and vocal range
  • Undo and redo, A/B compare and four colour themes
FreeGet it free in Aphotic Hub

How it works

Classic follows the singer and leaves the expression in. Underground snaps every note to the key for the locked sound modern rap vocals are built on.

Set the key and scale, pick a vocal range, and watch the live pitch display show what you sang, what came out and the note it's aiming for. Or send MIDI notes and TRINITY tunes to the notes you play.

Channel strip

IRON & GLASS

A British console preamp and EQ, into a tube optical compressor that holds a vocal steady without squashing it.

  • Mic and line input modes with iron drive
  • Fixed, Manual and Fix/Man compressor timing
  • VU meter for input, gain reduction and output
  • A/B compare and presets
$33Buy for $33

One-time purchase. Use your Hub email at checkout, then install in Aphotic Hub.

How it works

Iron is the console half: a Class-A preamp with transformer iron you can drive from clean to thick, then an EQ with curves fitted to the original circuit.

Glass is the tube optical compressor. Blend it in with the mix knob, keep the low end from pumping it with the sidechain high-pass, and put it before or after the EQ.

Tube limiter New

ABYSS DUAL

A two-channel tube limiter in a rack unit. A smooth optical limiter and a fast FET limiter work in a row on each side: what the first one misses, the second one catches.

  • OPTO: slow, smooth and deep (10:1), with a sidechain cut so bass does not pump it
  • FET: fast and firm (20:1), attack from 0.1 to 10 ms, release from 10 ms to 2 s, and a CLIP position
  • A tube stage that stays clean at normal levels and adds colour as you push it
  • Dual, Link or Max stereo modes; two VU meters with real VU movement and LED bars
  • MIX for parallel limiting, AUTO GAIN to keep the level steady, DELTA to hear what the limiters remove
  • 20 presets, an analyzer panel, oversampling up to 8x, resizable interface

Compressor New

LURE

Two kinds of compressor in one rack. A fast FET compressor catches the peaks, then a smooth optical leveler evens out the level. Use both, one alone, or swap their order.

  • FET compressor: attack from about 0.1 to 2 ms, release from 45 ms to 1.1 s, ratios 4, 8, 12 and 20, and all buttons in
  • Ratios tighten on held notes and the release slows after long passages, as on classic units of this kind
  • Optical leveler: Compress or Limit; a short hit is let go in milliseconds, a long passage in seconds
  • Emphasis, a sidechain high-pass and stereo link, so bass does not pump it
  • Mix for parallel compression; swap the order of the two units with one click
  • Two VU meters with real VU movement, showing gain reduction or output
  • 15 presets, oversampling up to 8x; zero latency, resizable interface

Preamp saturator New

TRENCH

One knob of preamp colour. DRIVE pushes your sound into a two-stage preamp and brings the level back, so you hear thickness and warm harmonics, not a jump in volume.

  • Transformer stage that works on the low end only: bass thickens first, everything above passes untouched
  • Amplifier stage whose harmonics follow how loud you play; highs reach it softer, so esses stay clean
  • Level-matched: a vocal keeps its loudness from 0 to 100% while its loudest peaks are tamed
  • Input, Output and Mix; High Pass and Low Pass that sit before or after the preamp; Phase
  • Oversampled inside, zero latency, click-free automation, resizable interface

How many computers can I use?

Each plug-in activates on up to 3 of your computers. To free a slot, deactivate a computer on the Hub's Account page (up to 3 moves per plug-in each month; email us if you need more).

Which DAWs and systems work?

Any DAW that loads VST3 plug-ins on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). On a Mac (macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon or Intel) TRINITY, IRON & GLASS, AIR, SOPE, UNDERTOW and SANTA come as VST3 and AU, so they also work in Logic Pro and GarageBand. ZION, HADAL and ABYSS DUAL are Windows only for now.

My computer warns me about the installer.

The Hub isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway once.

Can I get a refund?

If a plug-in doesn't work on a computer that meets its requirements and we can't fix it, contact us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund you. The details are in the Terms of Service.

Who handles the payment?

Gumroad is the seller of record. It takes the payment, adds any sales tax or VAT for your country and sends your receipt. Aphotic Audio never sees your card details.
