Midrange EQ New

EQ-5

A three-section midrange equalizer in the tradition of the classic valve units: lift the body with the low-mid peak, take out the box with the dip, bring the voice forward with the high-mid peak. The sections meet like a passive network, so they shape each other instead of cancelling.

  • Low-mid peak at 200, 300, 500, 700 Hz or 1 kHz, up to +10.7 dB; a wide, gentle bell
  • Dip from 200 Hz to 7 kHz in eleven steps, down to -11 dB
  • High-mid peak at 1.5, 2, 3, 4 or 5 kHz, up to +8.9 dB, narrowing as it goes up
  • A valve make-up stage and output transformer: a soft second harmonic and bass that thickens when pushed; Analog Drive for more, or switch the colour off for a clean EQ
  • EQ IN and POWER switch without clicks; POWER off passes your audio untouched
  • SETUP: live response curve, input and output trim, level match, oversampling, window size 75 to 200 %; 17 presets

De-esser New

SPLIT

A de-esser that listens for the balance between the S band and the rest of the voice, not for level. A whisper and a shout are treated the same, so there is no threshold to chase: set how much it may take and how hard it gets there, and every S comes down by the same measure.

  • Key filter, high pass or band pass, from 500 Hz to 15 kHz with FOCUS; KEY LISTEN to hear exactly what it hears
  • RANGE up to 36 dB (it never takes more), RATIO 2:1 to 20:1, RELEASE 1 to 100 ms
  • HF ONLY turns down only the highs, and SHIFT moves that band up to an octave either way; or let the whole voice dip
  • A 1.15 ms look-ahead catches the start of every S; steady hiss and room noise between words are left alone
  • A live display: the key filter, the reduction across the spectrum as it happens, stereo input and output meters and gain reduction
  • 16 presets for vocals, speech, instruments and the mix bus, A/B and undo

I used a different email at checkout.

Open Aphotic Hub, go to Library and paste the licence key from your Gumroad receipt under Bought on Gumroad with another email?. The plug-in moves into your account straight away. Stuck? Email aphoticsupport@gmail.com with your order number.

How many computers can I use?

Each plug-in activates on up to 3 of your computers. To free a slot, deactivate a computer on the Hub's Account page (up to 3 moves per plug-in each month; email us if you need more).

Which DAWs and systems work?

Any DAW that loads VST3 plug-ins on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). On a Mac (macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon or Intel) TRINITY, IRON & GLASS, AIR, SOPE, UNDERTOW, SANTA, HADAL, ZION, LURE, VANTA VOX, SOUNDING, JERMY, ABYSS DUAL, TUBE 50, EQ-5, PHOTIC and WAVY come as VST3 and AU, so they also work in Logic Pro and GarageBand.

My computer warns me about the installer.

The Hub isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway once.

Can I get a refund?

If a plug-in doesn't work on a computer that meets its requirements and we can't fix it, contact us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund you. The details are in the Terms of Service.
