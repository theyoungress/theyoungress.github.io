WAVY

Drop any recording on WAVY and it hands you the notes as MIDI: a sung hook becomes a synth lead, a sample's chords become a piano part, a drum loop becomes a pattern. Drag the notes straight into your DAW.

  • Four listeners: Melody (a singer or a lead), Bass, Chords (piano, guitar) and Drums (kick, snare, hats, toms and cymbals as General MIDI notes)
  • Drag the notes into your playlist or piano roll, or save a .mid file
  • Sensitivity, minimum note length, quantize (1/32 to 1/4) and heard or fixed velocity change the catch at once
  • Notes it is unsure of are shown fainter, so you know what to check by ear
  • The MIDI follows your DAW's tempo, and every mode's notes are saved with your project
  • Reads WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG and M4A
