Before you report

        
    
          
  • Update it in Aphotic Hub: the bug may already be fixed. Library shows Update when a new version is out.
    • 
          
  • Rescan plug-ins in your DAW, then restart it.
    • 
          
  • Try it in a new, empty project with only this plug-in on one track.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Attach to your email

        
    
          
  • A screenshot of the problem, or a short screen recording with sound.
    • 
          
  • For a crash, the crash report. Windows: open Event Viewer, then Windows Logs > Application, and look for an error that names your DAW. Mac: open Console, then Crash Reports.
    • 
          
  • A short audio clip if something sounds wrong.
    •
