VANTA VOX

Everything a vocal needs, in one unit: pitch correction and harmonies, dynamics, EQ, saturation and doubling, reverb and delay, glitch and a vocoder, and the sound of 25 classic microphones. Five modules in any order, each able to work on part of the voice or in parallel, six scenes you switch on the beat, motion, and Analyze my vocal, which listens to your recording and proposes settings with a reason for each. 55 presets from trap and rap to pop, R&B, harmony stacks and pitched effects.

  • Five modules: Tune, Dynamics, Tone, SFX and Space, in any order. Each can work on the middle, the sides, the lows or the highs, and be blended in parallel; Space can work as a send
  • Low Latency for recording (no added delay, about 1.5 ms with pitch correction in; pitch shift and formant add about 22 ms) and Studio for mixing; the real delay is shown, explained and reported to your DAW
  • Tune: pitch correction to a key and scale or to the notes you play on MIDI; pitch shift and formant; up to three harmony voices in the key or on held notes, each panned; a vocoder
  • Dynamics: cleanup (plosives, mouth clicks), gate, de-esser and two compressors with Opto, FET and Vari-Mu models. Tone: a nine-point EQ, Mud, Nasal and Harsh that act only on too much, Warmth, Clarity and Air
  • Mic: the sound of 25 classic microphones over your voice, named by their model numbers (VX-47, VX-67, VX-251, VX-800, VX-87 and more), with your own mic taken off first; polar pattern, Weight, Capsule, Color and the circuit (tube, FET, solid state, transformer)
  • SFX and Space: saturation up to Transformer and Distortion, doubler, chorus, width, lo-fi (Megaphone, Bit Crusher, Vinyl), reverb and delay with ducking, Glitch (Stutter, Reverse, Freeze, Tape Stop) and a 16-step sequencer
  • Analyze my vocal; six Scenes switched by automation; Motion (two LFOs and an envelope); Level Match; Auto Input Level; Simple, Advanced and Help modes; A and B, undo, a safety limiter; resizable interface
  • Clean at every step: every setting glides sample by sample, so nothing clicks when you move, switch or automate it; Studio saturation is oversampled and anti-aliased; the de-esser tells esses from bright vowels
  • MIDI (tuning, harmonies and the vocoder on the notes you play) works in VST3 hosts; in Logic the Audio Unit stays an audio effect
