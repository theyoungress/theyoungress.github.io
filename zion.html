Reverb

ZION

A reverb that draws the space you are building and keeps your mix clear while it blooms. Turn a knob and the display shows the gap, the reflections, the swell and the decay.

  • Six spaces: Hall, Room, Plate, Chamber, Cloud and Vintage, from 0.2 to 30 seconds
  • A live display, a five-band reverb EQ and a decay EQ you drag
  • Clear, Duck and Gate keep the mix clean, with a sidechain input; tempo-locked Pulse, Onset and Length
  • Halo shimmer an octave up, a fifth up or an octave down; Drift, Hold, Mono Lows and Guard
  • 71 presets with search and favourites, A/B morph, Inspire, undo
  • Zero latency, low CPU, resizable interface
