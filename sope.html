SOPE

A semi-modular analog-style synth you can play the moment it opens, and rewire when you want more. Drag from one jack to another and a cable hangs between them.

  • Two oscillators with hard sync, a mixer that overdrives and a four-pole ladder filter
  • 37 patch points, saved with your project and your presets
  • LFO, sample & hold, spring reverb; arpeggiator and 256-note sequencer with rests, ties, swing and tap tempo
  • 34 presets, A/B compare, keys that light up as you play
  • Low CPU, resizable interface
