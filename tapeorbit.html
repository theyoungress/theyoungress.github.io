TAPE ORBIT

A three-head tape echo with a spring reverb beside it, built like the classic tape echo units of the 1970s: warm repeats, pitch swoops when you move the tape speed, and self-oscillation you can play.

  • A moving tape with three playback heads: turn Repeat Rate and the motor takes about a second to catch up, so the echoes swoop in pitch like the old machines
  • A 12-position mode selector: four echo only, seven echo and spring, one spring only
  • Intensity from a single slap to endless repeats and controlled self-oscillation
  • Warm tape saturation, wow and flutter, Bass and Treble on the echo only, a VU meter and a PEAK lamp
  • A dispersive three-spring reverb, tempo sync, mono or stereo; a calibration drawer for wow, flutter, tape age, hiss, dropouts and more
  • 16 presets plus your own, zero latency, resizable interface
