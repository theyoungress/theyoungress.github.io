Metering New

SOUNDING

A meter for the whole mix. See how loud it is against a target, where its peaks are, how wide it is and where its energy sits, all on one screen. It only measures: the sound passes through untouched.

  • Loudness to ITU-R BS.1770-4 and EBU R 128 on a dial with your target: momentary, short-term, integrated, loudness range and true peak
  • Targets for streaming, podcasts and broadcast, or type your own; a history of the whole session
  • Spectrum standing on a waterfall spectrogram with the same frequency scale; 3D views as well
  • Stereo dial: a vectorscope with balance and correlation on its rim
  • Level meters with true or sample peak, RMS and crest, from mono to 7.1.2
  • Voice clarity: send the vocal to the sidechain and see how clearly it stands out from the mix
