UNDERTOW

Turns any melody, vocal or pad into a background layer of itself: an octave up, at half speed, broken into grains, moving on the beat and sitting in its own reverb.

  • Octave switch (+1, +2) and pitch in semitones, with a pitch shifter that keeps held notes steady
  • Grains: a cloud cut from the last few seconds, free or on the beat. Surge makes it wilder
  • Half speed at 1/2 or 1/4, in a loop that restarts on the beat
  • Six volume shapes and autopan in time with your song; width from mono to wide
  • Four reverbs: Classic, Plate, Room and Shimmer
  • 16 presets, and Generate rolls a new layer in one click
