Dual reverb pedal New

HADAL

Two reverbs in one pedal. Pick from twelve machines, run one into the other or side by side, and switch between two complete sounds with a footswitch.

  • Twelve machines: Room, Hall, Chamber, Plate, Spring, Impulse, Cloud, Shimmer, Swell, Choir, Tape and Nonlinear
  • Two engines in series or in parallel, each with its own machine and knobs
  • Footswitches A and B hold two sounds; Infinite freezes the reverb while you keep playing
  • Impulse plays six built-in spaces or your own WAV impulse responses
  • Made for vocals: Duck, Low Cut and De-Ess work on the reverb only, so the words stay clear
  • 45 presets with Pop Vocals and Rap Vocals banks, MIDI learn and a LAB panel with live meters; zero latency, resizable interface
